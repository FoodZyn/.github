# Code of Conduct

FoodZyn connects people across a whole industry — from family farms to Michelin
kitchens. We expect the same respect in how we build it.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive, in code review as everywhere else.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Welcome people of every background, experience level and role.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional kitchen or office.

## Scope

This code applies in all FoodZyn repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions
and any other space where you represent FoodZyn.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to an owner of the FoodZyn organization.
Reports are handled confidentially. Owners may remove comments, commits or
contributions and may restrict access for anyone who breaks this code.
