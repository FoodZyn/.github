# Security Policy

FoodZyn is meant to hold the business data of foodservice and trade businesses:
price lists, documents, orders and the people who work with them. We take its
security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Write to [security@foodzyn.com](mailto:security@foodzyn.com) and include:

- what is affected (repository, URL, app version),
- steps to reproduce,
- the impact you expect.

We acknowledge reports within three working days and keep you informed until the
issue is fixed. Please give us reasonable time to fix it before you disclose it.

## Supported versions

Only the latest deployed version of FoodZyn receives security fixes.
